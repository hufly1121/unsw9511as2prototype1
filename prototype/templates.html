Document Templates

Ready-to-use complaint letters and forms. Fill in the highlighted fields and send — no legal knowledge required.

💡
How to use these templates

Click Preview to see the full letter, then click Use This Template to copy it. Fields shown in yellow need to be replaced with your own information.

Most Popular

Refund Request Letter

Use this when you want to formally request a refund for a faulty product and the seller has not responded satisfactorily.

Escalation

Formal Complaint Letter

Use this if the seller has refused your initial request and you want to escalate the complaint with stronger language.

Service Issue

Service Complaint Letter

For when a paid service (repair, installation, delivery) was not delivered to the standard you were promised.

Billing

Billing Dispute Letter

Use this to formally dispute an incorrect charge, double billing, or an unauthorised transaction with a business.

📬
Send by email or post

Send your letter to the business's customer service email or postal address. Always keep a copy for your records. If sending by post, use registered mail so you have proof of delivery.

📄 Refund Request Letter

[Your Full Name]
[Your Address]
[Date]

The Manager / Customer Service
[Business Name]
[Business Address]

Subject: Request for Refund — Faulty Product

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to formally request a full refund for a product I purchased from your store on [date of purchase]. The item in question is a [product name and model], purchased for $[amount] with receipt number [receipt number].

Since [date problem noticed], the product has [describe the fault clearly — e.g. "stopped working entirely" / "does not perform as advertised"]. This constitutes a major failure under the Australian Consumer Law (Competition and Consumer Act 2010, Schedule 2).

Under Australian Consumer Law, I am entitled to choose the remedy I prefer when a product has a major failure. I am requesting a full refund of $[amount].

Please respond to this request within 14 days of receiving this letter. If I do not receive a satisfactory response, I will escalate this matter to [NSW Fair Trading / Consumer Affairs Victoria / relevant state body] and/or the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC).

I have attached [list what you're attaching: receipt, photos, etc.] as supporting evidence.

Yours sincerely,

[Your Name]
[Phone Number]
[Email Address]

📄 Formal Complaint Letter

[Your Full Name]
[Your Address]
[Date]

The Manager / Customer Service
[Business Name]

Subject: Formal Complaint and Notice of Intention to Escalate

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing as a follow-up to my previous correspondence dated [date of first contact], regarding a [faulty product / poor service / incorrect billing]. To date, I have not received a satisfactory response from your business.

I wish to make clear that under the Australian Consumer Law, I have statutory rights that cannot be excluded by any business policy, including "no refund" signs or policies. My entitlement to a remedy exists independently of your store's policies.

I am formally requesting that you provide [refund / replacement / repair] within 7 days of this notice.

Should you fail to respond or refuse this request, I will immediately lodge a formal complaint with [relevant Fair Trading office] and report your business to the ACCC for potential non-compliance with Australian Consumer Law.

Yours sincerely,

[Your Name]
[Contact Details]

📄 Service Complaint Letter

[Your Full Name]
[Your Address]
[Date]

The Manager
[Business Name]

Subject: Complaint Regarding Service Quality

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to formally complain about the service I received on [date of service]. I engaged your business to [describe the service, e.g. "repair my washing machine"] for the agreed price of $[amount].

The service was not performed with due care and skill as required under the Australian Consumer Law. Specifically, [describe exactly what went wrong — e.g. "the repair was incomplete and the machine still does not work"].

Under Australian Consumer Law, services must be provided with due care and skill, and must be reasonably fit for the purpose. I am requesting that you [re-do the work at no charge / provide a refund of $[amount]] within 14 days.

Yours sincerely,

[Your Name]
[Contact Details]

📄 Billing Dispute Letter

[Your Full Name]
[Your Address]
[Date]

Customer Service / Billing Department
[Business Name]

Subject: Billing Dispute — Reference [Invoice/Order Number]

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to dispute a charge on my account. On [date], I was charged $[amount] by your business. This charge is incorrect because [explain why — e.g. "I was charged twice for the same transaction" / "I did not authorise this charge" / "the amount differs from what was agreed"].

I am requesting an immediate correction of this billing error and a refund of $[amount] to [my original payment method / my account].

Please respond within 7 days. If this is not resolved promptly, I will report this matter to my bank and to the relevant consumer protection authority.

Yours sincerely,

[Your Name]
[Contact Details]
