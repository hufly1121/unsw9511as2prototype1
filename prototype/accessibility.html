♿ Our Accessibility Commitment

Consumer Rights Hub is designed to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards. We are committed to ensuring equal access for all users, including those with:

  • Visual impairments or low vision
  • Motor or mobility impairments
  • Cognitive or learning differences
  • Hearing impairments
  • Age-related accessibility needs
✅
Designed for elderly users

This website has been specifically designed with older users in mind — including large default text, high contrast options, simple navigation, and plain English content.
